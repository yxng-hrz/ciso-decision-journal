# Annual CISO Letter to the Board — 2026

Dear members of the Board,

This year tested us. A ransomware attack in February encrypted part of our production. We restored service in 52 hours without paying, met every regulatory deadline and kept our customers' trust. That outcome came from decisions taken a year earlier — MFA, immutable backups, a crisis process — and it also exposed what we had consciously accepted: **we detect too slowly and we recover too slowly**.

**Where we stand.** Our controls reduce aggregate cyber exposure by about a third. One residual risk remains critical — a prolonged platform outage — and four are high. NIS2 readiness is 70%, DORA contractual readiness 55%.

**What I ask of you.**
1. Close the detection gap: the MDR service goes live in January; the accepted risk on SIEM has expired and must not be renewed by default.
2. Fund the second-region warm standby: we promise customers a 4-hour recovery we cannot deliver today.
3. Hold management accountable for supplier security: our weakest NIS2 area.

**How to read our reports.** Security investments are risk reduction, not compliance cost. Each one is presented with its expected loss avoided and its return. Each accepted risk has an owner and an end date.

Cyber risk will never be zero. My commitment is that it remains **visible, measured and consciously decided**.

— CISO, Helios Docs
