# Company Context — Helios Docs (fictional)

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Industry | B2B SaaS — document management and e-signature |
| Size | 120 employees, ~€18M annual recurring revenue |
| Customers | SMEs, public-sector bodies and a growing base of **financial-sector** clients |
| Hosting | EU region of a hyperscale cloud provider (multi-AZ, single provider) |
| Data | Personal data, contracts, HR and financial documents of customers |
| Security team | CISO + 2 security engineers; detection outsourced to an MDR from 2027 |

## Regulatory perimeter

- **GDPR** — processor for customer data, controller for its own HR and marketing data.
- **NIS2** — likely in scope as an *important entity* (medium-sized digital provider); scope to be confirmed under the national transposition. Management bodies must approve and oversee cybersecurity measures (Art. 20).
- **DORA** — not directly regulated, but as an **ICT third-party service provider** to financial entities the company must accept DORA contractual requirements (Art. 30): audit rights, incident notification, exit support, resilience testing participation.

## Strategic constraints

- Strong growth pressure and a product team shipping weekly.
- Security budget ≈ 6% of the IT budget, under board scrutiny.
- Security questionnaires and due diligence have become a **sales prerequisite**.
- Customer trust is the product: a breach is an existential commercial risk.

## CISO mission

Protect the organization while enabling growth: make cyber risk visible, decide explicitly what is reduced, transferred or accepted, and prove it to customers, auditors and the board.
